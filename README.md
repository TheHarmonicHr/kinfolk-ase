# Àṣẹ ("ah-sheh")

**A capstone design research project by Harmony Simpson**  
IXD Program · Final Presentation: June 11, 2026

---

## What is Àṣẹ?

Àṣẹ is a private, physical-first memory system designed to lower the labor of documenting oral history and protect Black family data from corporate erasure.

The project asks: *what does it mean to design the onboarding experience for the family genealogist?*

In Yoruba tradition, àṣẹ is the divine force that flows through all living things and connects us to our ancestors. It is said at the end of prayers the way others say "amen." It is the power to make things happen. This project borrows that name deliberately — memory preservation, at its core, is an act of calling the past forward into the present.

---

## The Research

- **17 survey participants**
- **8 in-depth interviews** across multiple generations
- Focus: African American oral history, sensory memory, data sovereignty, and the lived experience of family archiving

Key finding: memory in this community does not live in files. It lives in a cast iron skillet, wind chimes, the smell of Sunday morning church, a grandmother's hour-long phone call that nobody recorded.

---

## The Problem

> "There is a definitive lack of a point person. Different people have fragments — pieces of photos or memories — but there isn't a 'team' ensuring history gets to the next generation."  
> — Tariq Edwards, Interview Participant

Existing platforms (iCloud, Google Photos, Facebook) were built for general consumer use — not for communities whose relationship to data is shaped by histories of surveillance, dispossession, and erasure. They are experienced as cold, costly, and structurally untrustworthy.

This is not a storage problem. It is a translation problem.

---

## Design Directions

**The Oral Map** — A microphone captures a spoken story and renders a living family constellation in real time. No story leaves the room.

**The Ancestral Vessel** — A weighted physical object you ask a question aloud. It draws from uploaded family data to return qualitative wisdom. Precedent: Paschal Beverly Randolph's magic mirror, 1858.

**The Ancestral OS** — A private, local operating system for family history that only activates when a physical heirloom is placed on a sensor. Zero cloud. The hardware is the vault.

---

## On Lineage

During this research I traced my own genealogy and discovered my great-great-great grandfather: **Paschal Beverly Randolph** (1825–1875) — a Black abolitionist, physician, novelist, and founder of the first Rosicrucian order in the United States. His spiritual practice centered on a physical object — the "magic mirror" — used to access ancestral wisdom and inner vision.

He nearly did not survive in my family's memory.  
He is now the clearest evidence I have for why this project is urgent.
